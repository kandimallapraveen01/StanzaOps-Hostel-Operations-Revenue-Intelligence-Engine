# Problem Statement

## Project Name

StanzaOps: Hostel Operations & Revenue Intelligence Engine

---

## Background

Hostel management involves handling large amounts of resident information, room allocation details, occupancy records, and payment transactions. Many hostel operations still depend on manual registers or scattered records, making data management difficult and time-consuming.

---

## Problem

The existing manual hostel management process creates several challenges:

- Difficulty maintaining accurate resident records
- Lack of centralized data storage
- Challenges in tracking room and bed occupancy
- Difficulty monitoring payment collection and pending payments
- Time-consuming report generation
- Limited visibility into hostel performance and revenue trends

Due to these challenges, hostel administrators cannot easily access real-time insights for effective decision-making.

---

## Proposed Solution

StanzaOps provides a data-driven hostel operations and revenue intelligence solution.

The system organizes resident, occupancy, and payment data into a structured dataset and uses SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze operational performance and generate meaningful business insights.

---

## Project Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Create a centralized hostel management dataset
- Analyse occupancy and room utilization
- Monitor payment and revenue performance
- Generate important business KPIs
- Build an interactive dashboard for decision-making

---

## Expected Outcome

The project helps hostel management improve operational efficiency by providing:

- Better data organization
- Faster reporting
- Improved payment tracking
- Clear occupancy insights
- Data-driven decision support
